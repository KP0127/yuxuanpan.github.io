01 Research

Questions grounded in everyday choices.

My research connects consumer behavior with consequential questions in food systems, health, marketing, and regulation. I combine large-scale consumer and product data with applied econometrics and machine learning.

01

Food, health & wellbeing

How economic shocks, food environments, and purchasing patterns affect nutrition, food security, and mental health.

02

Markets & consumer policy

How labels, sustainability claims, regulation, and pricing influence demand and market outcomes.

03

Data science for decisions

How machine learning and new product data can measure complex markets and clarify consumer choices.
