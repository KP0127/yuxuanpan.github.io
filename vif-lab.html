          1. Diagnose
          
Use estat vif after regress. VIF above 5 is worth discussion; VIF above 10 is often treated as serious.

        

        

          2. Explain
          
Multicollinearity does not bias OLS coefficients by itself. It inflates variance, so coefficients become unstable and standard errors grow.

        

        

          3. Respond
          
Try theory-driven variable selection, combining overlapping measures, centering interactions, or collecting more data. Do not drop controls mechanically.
